FluentEng Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FluentEng handles information when you use the mobile application.

Information collected by FluentEng

FluentEng does not require an account and does not intentionally collect, store, sell, or share personal information about its users.

Network access and third-party content

FluentEng uses an internet connection to load learning content and to display public web pages and articles from third-party websites, including BBC pages. When third-party pages are loaded, those websites may receive standard technical information such as your IP address, browser or device information, and may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own privacy policies.

FluentEng does not control the privacy practices of those third-party websites. Users should review the privacy policies of the relevant third-party services for more information.

Advertising

FluentEng does not operate its own advertising service and does not use personal information for advertising. Third-party web pages displayed inside or opened from the app may contain their own advertising or tracking technologies, subject to the policies of those third parties.

Analytics

FluentEng does not currently use an in-app analytics service to build user profiles or track users across apps or websites.

Data retention

Because FluentEng does not intentionally collect personal information on its own servers, it does not maintain a personal-data retention database for app users.

Children's privacy

FluentEng is a general educational tool and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

BBC disclaimer

FluentEng is an independent educational application. It is not an official BBC application and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the BBC. BBC names, trademarks, content, and webpages remain the property of their respective rights holders.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published at this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, please use the developer contact information shown on FluentEng's Google Play listing.
